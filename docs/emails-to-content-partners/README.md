# Emails to Content Partners

Ready-to-send email templates for onboarding and communicating with content partners.

Copy, fill in the bracketed fields, and send. Keep the tone conversational — these are indie creators, not studios.

---

## Templates

| File | When to use |
|---|---|
| [01-initial-outreach.md](01-initial-outreach.md) | First contact — reaching out to a creator you want on the platform |
| [02-asset-request.md](02-asset-request.md) | Following up to request their assets after they've said yes |
| [03-content-live.md](03-content-live.md) | Letting them know their content is live on Peep |
| [04-follow-up.md](04-follow-up.md) | Checking in if they haven't responded in a week |
