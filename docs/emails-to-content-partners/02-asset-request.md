# 02 — Asset Request

Use after a creator has agreed to be on the platform. Tells them exactly what to send.

---

**Subject:** Peep — what we need from you

Hey [Name],

Great to have you on board. Here's exactly what we need to get [Film/Show/Podcast] live on Peep.

---

**Poster** *(card artwork shown in browse rows)*
- 1200 × 1800px, JPG or WebP
- Bake your title/logo into it — we don't overlay any text on top

**Hero Image** *(full-screen banner on the home page — featured titles only)*
- 2560 × 1440px, JPG or WebP, under 500KB
- Same deal — title baked in, main subject on the right side of frame

**Video File**
- MP4, 1080p minimum

**Trailer** *(optional but recommended)*
- MP4 or YouTube link, 60–180 seconds

**Podcast Artwork** *(podcasts only)*
- 1400 × 1400px square — same file you'd submit to Spotify works perfectly

---

Also include:
- Title
- Year
- Runtime (e.g. "42 min")
- Rating (TV-MA, NR, R, etc.)
- Short description (1–2 sentences)
- Genre / tags (e.g. "Horror, Dark Comedy, Indie")

You can send everything to [email] or share a Drive/Dropbox link — whatever's easiest.

Once we have it we'll get it uploaded and send you a link to review before it goes live.

— [Your name]
Peep · watchpeep.tv
