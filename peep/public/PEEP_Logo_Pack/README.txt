PEEP Logo Pack
==============
Core palette:
PEEP Yellow: #FFD600
Black:       #000000
White:       #FFFFFF

Files:
- peep-logo-primary.svg: stacked primary lockup
- peep-logo-horizontal.svg: navbar/header lockup
- peep-wordmark-white.svg
- peep-wordmark-yellow.svg
- peep-eyes.svg: standalone screen + eyes mark
- peep-icon.svg: square app/social icon
- favicon.svg
- favicon-16.png / favicon-32.png
- peep-app-icon-512.png / peep-app-icon-1024.png
- PNG exports of the major logos

Usage:
Keep clear space around the mark. Use yellow/black as the primary identity.
For dark app surfaces, use the white wordmark. Avoid recoloring the eyes mark
outside the official yellow/black/white palette.
