# Peep Creator Revenue Share

## How It Works

Every month, **50% of Peep's total revenue goes into a creator pool**. Your cut of that pool is based on how much of the platform's total watch time your content drove that month.

The per-minute rate is never fixed — it grows as Peep grows. More subscribers means more revenue, which means a bigger pool and more money per watch minute. As a creator, you benefit directly when the platform grows.

---

## The Formula

```
Creator Pool  =  Total Revenue × 50%
Your Payout   =  Creator Pool × (Your Watch Minutes ÷ Total Watch Minutes on Platform)
```

---

## Example

| | |
|---|---|
| Peep revenue this month | $2,000 |
| Creator pool (50%) | $1,000 |
| Your content's share of watch time | 30% |
| **Your payout** | **$300** |

Same content, bigger month:

| | |
|---|---|
| Peep revenue this month | $5,000 |
| Creator pool (50%) | $2,500 |
| Your content's share of watch time | 30% |
| **Your payout** | **$750** |

---

## What Counts as a Watch

To keep payouts fair and accurate:

- A viewing session must reach at least **25% of an episode's runtime** to count toward your total
- Each viewer only counts once per episode per month, regardless of how many times they rewatch it
- Watch time is tracked automatically — no action needed on your part

---

## Payment Schedule

Payments go out on the **15th of each month** for the previous month's watch data.

Example: April watch time is paid out on May 15th.
